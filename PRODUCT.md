# RV University Placements Redesign

## Overview
A competition redesign of the official RV University (RVU) Placements portal (`https://rvu.edu.in/placements/`). The redesign elevates the user experience for three core stakeholders: **Corporate Recruiters**, **Students**, and **Parents**.

## Brand Identity & Colors
- **RVU Primary Slate Blue**: `#233039`
- **RVU Warm College Gold**: `#d3a957`
- **RVU Soft Tint Neutral**: `#ebf1f4`
- **RVU Canvas Neutral**: `#f7fafc` / `#ffffff`
- **RVU Ink / Heading**: `#151e24`

## Target Stakeholders & Features
1. **Corporate Recruiters**:
   - School-by-School Talent Directory with student strengths across 6 academic schools.
   - Dual-track infinite logo marquee with 250+ enterprise recruiters.
   - Job Announcement Form (JAF) modal for campus drive scheduling.
2. **Students**:
   - 5-Stage Placement Stepper (Profiling -> Bootcamps -> Internships -> Drives -> Offers).
   - Searchable, category-filtered Placement Policy and Code of Conduct accordion drawer.
3. **Parents**:
   - Verified salary outcomes (₹43.5 LPA highest, ₹9.5 LPA average, 425+ offers, 25% multiple offers).
   - Trust and ROI pillars celebrating 80+ years of RV Educational Institutions heritage.
