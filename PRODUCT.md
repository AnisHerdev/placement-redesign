# Product: RV University Placements & Career Portal

## Register

brand

## Users
- **Students**: Seeking placement rules, training calendar, career opportunities, company eligibility criteria, recruitment process steps, and downloadable preparation resources. Context: mobile & desktop browsing during campus recruitment seasons.
- **Corporate Partners / Recruiters**: Looking to hire top talent across Engineering, Design, Business, Law, Liberal Arts, and Science. They need immediate access to batch profiles, key metrics, recruitment process guidelines, campus infrastructure details, and a streamlined "Host Campus Drive / Recruit Talent" action channel.
- **Parents**: Evaluating university return-on-investment, academic credibility, highest & average package benchmarks, recruiter prestige (Fortune 500, MNCs), safety, and career growth outcomes for prospective and current students.

## Product Purpose
Deliver an elite, trustworthy, modern, and high-converting Placements & Career Development web experience for RV University that wins the redesign competition by outperforming the legacy Divi site across accessibility, aesthetics, speed, stakeholder UX, and data clarity.

## Brand Personality
- **Prestigious & Trustworthy**: Backed by the 85+ year Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST) legacy.
- **Forward-Thinking & Innovative**: Interdisciplinary curriculum, cutting-edge labs, industry 4.0 readiness.
- **Empowering & Transparent**: Honest metrics, clear student trajectories, effortless recruiter onboarding.

## Anti-references
- **The Legacy Divi Template**: 467KB HTML bloated with 600+ wrapper divs, broken table layouts, 70 tracking scripts, unreadable contrast, and buried CTAs.
- **Generic AI Template Clichés**: Monotonous identical 3-column card grids, decorative gradient text, low-contrast washed-out grays, and tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows above every single title.
- **SaaS Clutter**: Over-hyped tech buzzwords that undermine academic gravitas.

## Design Principles
1. **Stakeholder Clarity First**: Dedicated contextual pathways for Students, Recruiters, and Parents without fracturing the overarching prestige.
2. **Data with Dignity**: Highlight compensation and placement statistics with context, clarity, and verified credibility rather than disembodied floating numbers.
3. **Frictionless Action**: Every stakeholder has an immediate, clear next step (Apply, Register Drive, Download Brochure, Contact CDCR).
4. **Accessible Excellence**: Strictly conform to WCAG 2.1 AA (minimum 4.5:1 contrast, keyboard accessible, fluid responsive layouts without horizontal scroll).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA compliant.
- High contrast ratios (body text ≥ 4.5:1, headers ≥ 3:1).
- Responsive down to 320px mobile viewport without horizontal table blowout.
- Screen-reader friendly semantic landmarks and descriptive image alt attributes.
